Services

Procurement Expertise, Built Around Your Project.

BuildWise manages the sourcing and procurement process for finish materials used in residential development projects across the United States. Services are scoped to each project, with optional capabilities available when needed.

Core service

Sourcing & Procurement Management

  • Supplier identification and quotation coordination
  • Product and specification comparisons
  • Budget-aware options
  • Purchase coordination and documentation
  • Approval tracking
  • Production status coordination

The final scope is defined in the project agreement.

Optional

Design & Finish Planning

Coordinate with your existing design team, or explore a full finish-selection and planning package through qualified design partners in Asia. Potential scope includes kitchens, cabinetry, vanities, bathrooms, flooring, tile, doors, fixtures, and coordinated finishes.

U.S. architects, engineers, designers, contractors, and local authorities retain responsibility for approvals within their respective roles.

Optional

Quality Control Coordination

Factory inspections, third-party product inspections, sampling, and testing can be arranged based on the products and project requirements. Third-party services are quoted separately.

Optional

Logistics & Delivery Coordination

Freight consolidation, international shipping, licensed customs-broker coordination, inland transportation, unloading, and job-site delivery can be coordinated through appropriate service providers when requested. These services are separately scoped and quoted; availability depends on project location and needs.

Additional services are available upon request and quoted separately based on project requirements.

How it works

A Clear Process From Project Plans to Product Delivery.

Every project starts with your plans, priorities, and budget. We define the procurement scope together and keep product decisions organized along the way.

01

Project Onboarding

Send architectural plans, available finish schedules, target budget, timeline, and project details. We review the scope and identify procurement priorities.

02

Design & Specifications

We coordinate with your architect or designer, or discuss optional design support, to define dimensions, materials, finishes, technical requirements, and approval responsibilities.

03

Sourcing & Quotation

We identify suitable manufacturing options and organize quotations, alternatives, specifications, and commercial information for your review.

04

Product Approval

You and your designated professionals approve the products, samples where applicable, specifications, and commercial terms before orders proceed.

05

Production & Quality Coordination

We coordinate production updates and arrange agreed inspections or quality checks as appropriate to the scope.

06

Shipping & Delivery Coordination

If requested, we coordinate logistics through qualified partners, including freight and U.S. delivery arrangements. Import responsibilities and customs requirements are addressed in the project’s commercial documentation.

About

Your U.S. Procurement Partner for Residential Development.

BuildWise was created around a straightforward idea: residential developers should have a more practical way to explore global manufacturing opportunities without having to manage the entire overseas sourcing process themselves.

We bring a U.S.-focused approach to procurement coordination, with attention to specifications, communication, budget alignment, and execution. We work alongside developers and their architects, designers, and builders, not in place of them.
